About AJF

Unleashing Potential with Creative Strategy

Welcome to the Abhay Jere Foundation, a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering communities, fostering innovation, and promoting sustainable growth. Our mission is to create lasting positive impact by supporting initiatives in all relevant social sectors like education, skilling, entrepreneurship, commerce & finance, healthcare, travel & tourism, research, technology, etc. We work towards building a future where everyone has the opportunity to thrive, regardless of their background or circumstances. This foundation is established in honor of Dr. Abhay Jere for furthering his commitment to transformative change at scale in our society. With a focus on making meaningful contributions, we collaborate with individuals, organizations, and communities to drive progress in areas that matter the most.
