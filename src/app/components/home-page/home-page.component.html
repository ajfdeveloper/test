About Abhay Jere Foundation

Welcome to the Abhay Jere Foundation, a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering communities, fostering innovation, and promoting sustainable growth. Our mission is to create lasting positive impact by supporting initiatives in all relevant social sectors like education, skilling, entrepreneurship, commerce & finance, healthcare, travel & tourism, research, technology, etc. We work towards building a future where everyone has the opportunity to thrive, regardless of their background or circumstances.

With a team of experienced professionals, we strive to achieve excellence in every project we undertake. Our mission is to empower communities through technology, collaboration, and forward-thinking strategies.

This foundation is established in honor of Dr. Abhay Jere for furthering his commitment to transformative change at scale in our society. With a focus on making meaningful contributions, we collaborate with individuals, organizations, and communities to drive progress in areas that matter the most.

OUR OBJECTIVES

  • Promoting Education & Skilling
    • 1. Enhance access to quality education for students across various socio-economic backgrounds.
    • 2. Develop and implement skill-based programs to improve employability.
    • 3. Introduce innovative learning methodologies and digital education tools.
  • Encouraging Entrepreneurship & Innovation
    • 1. Support aspiring entrepreneurs through mentorship, funding, and incubation programs.
    • 2. Foster a culture of innovation through hackathons, competitions, and startup accelerators.
    • 3. Partner with industry leaders to provide hands-on entrepreneurial training.
  • Collaboration with other Social Organisations
    • 1. Engage with relevant social organisations in a Hub-and-Spoke Model.
    • 2. Create a network of knowledge-sharing and resource distribution.
    • 3. Leverage partnerships to reach underserved communities effectively.
  • Teachers & Faculty Training Programs
    • 1. Organize workshops and training sessions to upskill educators and faculty members.
    • 2. Introduce modern teaching methodologies, including digital tools and experiential learning.
    • 3. Provide faculty with industry exposure to align education with real-world needs.
  • Healthcare & Community Services
    • 1. Support healthcare initiatives, including preventive care, telemedicine, and medical camps.
    • 2. Collaborate with hospitals and medical institutions to improve healthcare accessibility.
    • 3. Promote mental health awareness and well-being programs within communities.
  • Offering Various Services to the Community
    • 1. Provide career guidance, vocational training, and skill development for youth and women.
    • 2. Support social welfare initiatives, including environmental sustainability and rural development.
    • 3. Facilitate financial literacy programs and digital empowerment for marginalized sections.
