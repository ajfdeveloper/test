Dr. R. Venkateswaran

Dr. R. Venkateswaran is a highly accomplished professional with a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Washington State University, Pullman, WA, and a distinguished graduate of the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Mumbai. With over 30 years of experience in the IT industry, including more than 20 years as the Global Chief Information Officer (CIO) at Persistent Systems Limited, Dr. Venkateswaran has been at the forefront of technological innovation and organizational leadership. His expertise spans across IT strategy, digital transformation, and global technology management, having successfully led initiatives that have had a lasting impact on the IT landscape. Currently, Dr. Venkateswaran is a Professor of Practice at the prestigious Gokhale Institute of Politics and Economics, where he shares his wealth of knowledge and experience with the next generation of leaders.

As an advisor to the Abhay Jere Foundation, Dr. Venkateswaran brings his vast experience in technology and leadership to help shape and guide the foundation’s efforts in driving social impact, particularly in education, innovation, and sustainable growth.

Dr. Rahul Kulkarni

Dr. Rahul Kulkarni holds a Ph.D. in Microbiology from Pune University and has over 27 years of experience in the biotechnology industry. He has worked with some of the most renowned companies, including Serum Institute, Emcure Pharma, and Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories. His most recent role was as the Head of Regulatory Affairs at Sanofi, where he led regulatory strategies for a global healthcare leader.

With his vast expertise in pharma business, Dr. Kulkarni now serves as an Advisor to the Abhay Jere Foundation, where he applies his knowledge to help micro-entrepreneurs scale up and expand their businesses.

Shri. Adesh Gokhale

Shri. Adesh Gokhale is a seasoned professional with a distinguished career in technology and business leadership. Having spent several years with IBM, where he held key positions driving strategic initiatives and technological advancements, Adesh has developed a deep expertise in innovation, business transformation, and digital solutions.

Now, as an Advisor to the Abhay Jere Foundation, Adesh brings his wealth of experience to help guide the foundation’s initiatives. His insights into technology, leadership, and strategic growth are invaluable as the foundation continues to expand its impact in education, healthcare, and sustainable development. With a strong passion for social impact, Adesh is dedicated to supporting the foundation’s mission of empowering communities and driving positive change.

Ms.Shweta Shalini

Ms. Shweta Shalini is a prominent personality in state of Maharashtra. She serves as an advisor to the Chief Minister of Maharashtra. In her role as Executive Director of the Maharashtra Village Social Transformation Foundation (VSTF), a government initiative chaired by the Chief Minister, she has been instrumental in efforts to transform 1,000 villages across the state.

Ms. Shalini is also a successful entrepreneur, founding two IT startups and earning the title of Entrepreneur of the Year in 2011. She is also the Chief Evangelist, Investor, and Mentor of Billenium Divas, a women-focused angel fund, and serves as an advisor to NITI Aayog, the Government of India’s policy think tank.
