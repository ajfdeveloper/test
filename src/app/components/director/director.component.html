Mrs. Vaishali Jere

Director, Abhay Jere Foundation

At the heart of the Abhay Jere Foundation is Mrs. Vaishali Jere, our Director, whose leadership and vision inspire our work. With strong international experience and having worked in large global corporates, Ms. Vaishali strongly believes in the power of collective action. She brings a wealth of experience in community development and a passion for creating opportunities for growth and progress.

She leads with empathy, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to creating a more equitable society. Under her guidance, the foundation has expanded its reach and deepened its impact, touching the lives of countless individuals and communities across various sectors. Mrs. Jere's approach is centered on collaboration, empowering those around her, and making a lasting difference for generations to come.
